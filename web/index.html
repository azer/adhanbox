AdhanBox

E-ink prayer times and adhan for the Waveshare ESP32-S3-ePaper-1.54 (V2).

  1. Plug the board into this computer with a USB-C data cable.
  2. Click Install and pick the port that appears (often “USB JTAG/serial debug unit”).
  3. Wait about two minutes while the firmware and adhan audio are written.

Your browser can't talk to USB devices. Open this page in Chrome or Edge on a computer. This page must be opened over https.

Works in Chrome and Edge on macOS, Windows and Linux. Nothing to install.

After installing

  1. The board restarts and shows the welcome screen. Press any button.
  2. Scan the QR code with your phone to join the AdhanBox-XXXX network.
  3. Pick your Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz) and your city on the page that opens.

Prefer the terminal?

On macOS or Linux:

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/azer/adhanbox/main/install.sh | sh
